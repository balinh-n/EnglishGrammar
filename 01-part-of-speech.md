# Parts of Speech in English

## 1. What Is a Part of Speech?

A **part of speech** is a category that describes the **function of a word in a sentence**.

English traditionally has **8 main parts of speech**:

1. Noun
2. Pronoun
3. Verb
4. Adjective
5. Adverb
6. Preposition
7. Conjunction
8. Interjection

---

## 2. Summary of the 8 Parts of Speech

| Part of Speech | Function | Common Examples | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Noun** | Names a person, place, thing, or idea | teacher, city, book, happiness | The **teacher** is kind. |
| **Pronoun** | Replaces a noun | I, you, he, she, it, they | **She** is my friend. |
| **Verb** | Shows an action, state, or occurrence | run, eat, be, seem, become | They **work** every day. |
| **Adjective** | Describes or modifies a noun/pronoun | big, beautiful, smart, five | She has a **beautiful** house. |
| **Adverb** | Modifies a verb, adjective, adverb, or sentence | quickly, very, carefully, yesterday | He runs **quickly**. |
| **Preposition** | Shows a relationship between words | in, on, at, under, with, from | The book is **on** the table. |
| **Conjunction** | Connects words, phrases, or clauses | and, but, or, because, although | I was tired, **but** I worked. |
| **Interjection** | Expresses emotion or reaction | wow, oh, hey, ouch, wow | **Wow!** That's amazing. |

---

# 3. Nouns

A **noun** names a person, place, thing, animal, or idea.

### Examples

- **Person:** teacher, doctor, John
- **Place:** school, London, park
- **Thing:** book, computer, table
- **Animal:** dog, cat, elephant
- **Idea:** love, happiness, freedom

| Type | Explanation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Common noun** | General name | city, teacher, dog |
| **Proper noun** | Specific name; usually begins with a capital letter | London, Sarah, Vietnam |
| **Countable noun** | Can be counted | book, apple, student |
| **Uncountable noun** | Cannot normally be counted individually | water, information, advice |
| **Concrete noun** | Can be experienced through the senses | table, phone, music |
| **Abstract noun** | Names an idea, feeling, or quality | love, freedom, happiness |
| **Collective noun** | Refers to a group | team, family, committee |

**Example:**

> The **student** opened the **book**.

---

# 4. Pronouns

A **pronoun** replaces a noun to avoid repetition.

### Example

> Sarah is a teacher. **She** works at a school.

**She** replaces **Sarah**.

| Type | Examples | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | I, you, he, she, it, we, they | **She** is happy. |
| **Object** | me, you, him, her, it, us, them | He called **me**. |
| **Possessive** | mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs | This book is **mine**. |
| **Reflexive** | myself, yourself, himself, herself, ourselves | She hurt **herself**. |
| **Demonstrative** | this, that, these, those | **This** is my bag. |
| **Relative** | who, which, that, whose | The man **who** called is my uncle. |
| **Interrogative** | who, what, which, whose | **Who** is there? |
| **Indefinite** | someone, anyone, everyone, nothing | **Someone** called you. |

---

# 5. Verbs

A **verb** expresses an action, state, or occurrence.

### Action Verbs

> run, eat, write, speak, study, work

**Example:**

> She **writes** every day.

### Linking Verbs

Link the subject to information about the subject.

> be, seem, become, appear, feel, look, remain

**Example:**

> He **is** tired.

### Auxiliary / Helping Verbs

Help form tenses, questions, negatives, and passive voice.

> be, do, have

**Example:**

> She **is studying**.

### Modal Verbs

Show ability, possibility, permission, obligation, or advice.

> can, could, may, might, must, should, will, would

**Example:**

> You **should study**.

| Verb Type | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Action verb** | Shows an action | She **runs**. |
| **Linking verb** | Links subject to description | She **is** happy. |
| **Auxiliary verb** | Helps the main verb | She **is studying**. |
| **Modal verb** | Shows possibility, ability, obligation, etc. | She **can swim**. |

---

# 6. Adjectives

An **adjective** describes or modifies a noun or pronoun.

### Examples

> a **big** house

> a **beautiful** flower

> an **interesting** book

> She is **happy**.

| Function | Example |
|---|---|
| Describes size | a **large** house |
| Describes color | a **red** car |
| Describes quality | a **beautiful** painting |
| Describes number | **three** books |
| Describes opinion | an **interesting** movie |
| Describes condition | a **broken** chair |

### Common Position

**Before a noun:**

> a **beautiful** house

**After a linking verb:**

> The house is **beautiful**.

---

# 7. Adverbs

An **adverb** modifies a verb, adjective, another adverb, or sometimes a whole sentence.

| What It Modifies | Example |
|---|---|
| **Verb** | She speaks **slowly**. |
| **Adjective** | It is **very** cold. |
| **Adverb** | He runs **extremely** quickly. |
| **Sentence** | **Fortunately**, nobody was hurt. |

### Common Types of Adverbs

| Type | Question | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Manner** | How? | quickly, slowly, carefully |
| **Time** | When? | now, yesterday, soon |
| **Place** | Where? | here, there, everywhere |
| **Frequency** | How often? | always, often, sometimes, never |
| **Degree** | How much? | very, extremely, quite, too |

**Example:**

> She completed the work **carefully**.

---

# 8. Prepositions

A **preposition** shows the relationship between a noun/pronoun and another word.

### Common Prepositions

> in, on, at, by, for, from, to, with, about, under, over, between, among, through

| Relationship | Examples | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Place** | in, on, under, behind | The cat is **under** the table. |
| **Time** | at, on, in, before, after | I wake up **at** 7:00. |
| **Direction** | to, into, toward | She went **to** school. |
| **Source** | from | He is **from** Canada. |
| **Connection** | with | I went **with** my friend. |
| **Purpose** | for | This gift is **for** you. |

### Common Time Rules

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | Exact time | **at** 7:00 |
| **on** | Days and dates | **on** Monday |
| **in** | Months, years, periods | **in** July, **in** 2025 |

---

# 9. Conjunctions

A **conjunction** connects words, phrases, or clauses.

### Coordinating Conjunctions

**FANBOYS:**

> for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so

**Example:**

> I was tired, **but** I continued working.

### Subordinating Conjunctions

> because, although, if, when, while, since, unless, before, after

**Example:**

> I stayed home **because** it was raining.

### Correlative Conjunctions

> both...and  
> either...or  
> neither...nor  
> not only...but also

**Example:**

> **Both** Tom **and** Mary are students.

---

# 10. Interjections

An **interjection** expresses a sudden emotion, reaction, or feeling.

| Emotion | Interjection | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Surprise | wow, oh | **Wow!** That's amazing. |
| Pain | ouch | **Ouch!** That hurts. |
| Greeting | hey, hello | **Hey!** How are you? |
| Happiness | yay, hooray | **Yay!** We won! |
| Attention | hey, look | **Hey!** Listen to me. |
| Disappointment | oh no | **Oh no!** We missed the bus. |

Interjections are often followed by an **exclamation mark (!)**.

---

# 11. Parts of Speech in One Sentence

Consider this sentence:

> **Wow! She quickly opened the beautiful box on the table, and she smiled.**

| Word | Part of Speech | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Wow** | Interjection | Expresses surprise |
| **She** | Pronoun | Replaces a noun |
| **quickly** | Adverb | Describes how she opened it |
| **opened** | Verb | Shows the action |
| **the** | Determiner | Identifies the noun |
| **beautiful** | Adjective | Describes the box |
| **box** | Noun | Names a thing |
| **on** | Preposition | Shows the relationship between box and table |
| **the** | Determiner | Identifies the noun |
| **table** | Noun | Names a thing |
| **and** | Conjunction | Connects two clauses |
| **she** | Pronoun | Replaces a noun |
| **smiled** | Verb | Shows the action |

> **Note:** Modern grammar often treats words such as **the, a, an, this, my, some** as **determiners**, which are usually considered a separate category rather than one of the traditional eight parts of speech.

# 12. Quick Summary

| No. | Part of Speech | Main Question / Function | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Noun** | Who or what? | teacher, city, happiness |
| 2 | **Pronoun** | Replaces a noun | I, she, they, someone |
| 3 | **Verb** | What happens / what does someone do? | run, eat, be, seem |
| 4 | **Adjective** | What kind? Which one? How many? | big, red, beautiful |
| 5 | **Adverb** | How? When? Where? How often? | quickly, yesterday, here |
| 6 | **Preposition** | Shows relationship | in, on, at, under |
| 7 | **Conjunction** | Connects ideas | and, but, because |
| 8 | **Interjection** | Expresses emotion | wow, oh, ouch |

---

# 13. Easy Way to Remember

| Part of Speech | Think of It As |
|---|---|
| **Noun** | A name |
| **Pronoun** | A replacement for a noun |
| **Verb** | An action or state |
| **Adjective** | A description |
| **Adverb** | Information about an action or description |
| **Preposition** | A relationship |
| **Conjunction** | A connector |
| **Interjection** | An emotion or reaction |

## Key Point

A word's **part of speech depends on how it is used in a sentence**.

For example, **fast** can be an adjective or an adverb:

- He drives a **fast** car. → **Adjective**
- He drives **fast**. → **Adverb**
