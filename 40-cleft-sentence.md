# Summary: Cleft Sentences in English

## 1. What Is a Cleft Sentence?

A **cleft sentence** is a sentence divided into **two parts** to give special **emphasis** to a particular piece of information.

### Normal Sentence

> John broke the window.

### Cleft Sentence

> **It was John who broke the window.**

The cleft sentence emphasizes **John**.

---

## 2. Main Types of Cleft Sentences

| **Type** | **Main Function** | **Basic Structure** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **It-cleft** | Emphasizes a person, thing, place, time, etc. | It + be + emphasized element + who/that... | **It was John who called me.** |
| **Wh-cleft** | Emphasizes an action, thing, or idea | What + clause + be + emphasized element | **What I need is more time.** |
| **All-cleft** | Emphasizes the only thing that happened/is needed | All + clause + be + emphasized element | **All I need is some rest.** |
| **There-cleft** | Emphasizes the existence or presence of something | There + be + emphasized element + relative clause | **There was a man who helped me.** |
| **Reverse wh-cleft** | Puts the emphasized information first | Emphasized element + be + what-clause | **More time is what I need.** |

---

# 3. It-Cleft Sentences

The **it-cleft** is one of the most common cleft structures.

### Structure

> **It + be + emphasized information + who/that + remaining clause**

| **Information Emphasized** | **Example** |
|---|---|
| Person | **It was Sarah who called me.** |
| Thing | **It was the red car that he bought.** |
| Place | **It was in London that we met.** |
| Time | **It was yesterday that she arrived.** |
| Reason | **It was because he was tired that he left.** |

### Example

**Normal:**

> Sarah called me yesterday.

**Emphasizing Sarah:**

> **It was Sarah who called me yesterday.**

**Emphasizing yesterday:**

> **It was yesterday that Sarah called me.**

**Emphasizing the action/location:**

> **It was me that Sarah called.**

---

# 4. Who or That?

In it-cleft sentences, **who** is commonly used for people, while **that** can be used for people or things.

| **Emphasized Element** | **Common Connector** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| Person | **who** | It was **John who** helped me. |
| Person | **that** | It was **John that** helped me. |
| Thing | **that** | It was **the book that** I wanted. |
| Place/time | **that** | It was **here that** we met. |

### 📌 Note

In formal English, **who** is often preferred when emphasizing a person.

> **It was Maria who won the competition.**

---

# 5. Wh-Cleft Sentences

A **wh-cleft**, also called a **pseudo-cleft**, usually begins with **what**.

### Structure

> **What + clause + be + emphasized information**

| **Normal Sentence** | **Wh-Cleft Sentence** |
|---|---|
| I need more time. | **What I need is more time.** |
| She wants a new job. | **What she wants is a new job.** |
| He likes traveling. | **What he likes is traveling.** |
| We need a solution. | **What we need is a solution.** |
| They want an explanation. | **What they want is an explanation.** |

### Example

**Normal:**

> I need a break.

**Wh-cleft:**

> **What I need is a break.**

The sentence emphasizes **a break**.

---

# 6. All-Cleft Sentences

An **all-cleft** sentence uses **all** to emphasize that something is the only thing needed, wanted, or done.

### Structure

> **All + clause + be + emphasized information**

| **Example** | **Meaning** |
|---|---|
| **All I need is some sleep.** | Some sleep is the only thing I need. |
| **All she wants is an apology.** | An apology is the only thing she wants. |
| **All we need is more time.** | More time is the only thing we need. |
| **All he did was complain.** | Complaining was the only thing he did. |

---

# 7. Reverse Wh-Cleft

A **reverse wh-cleft** puts the emphasized information first.

### Structure

> **Emphasized information + be + what-clause**

| **Wh-Cleft** | **Reverse Wh-Cleft** |
|---|---|
| What I need is more time. | **More time is what I need.** |
| What she wants is a new car. | **A new car is what she wants.** |
| What we need is a solution. | **A solution is what we need.** |
| What he likes is classical music. | **Classical music is what he likes.** |

Both forms emphasize the information after **is** in the first version and the information at the beginning in the reverse version.

---

# 8. Cleft Sentences for Different Types of Information

| **Information** | **Normal Sentence** | **Cleft Sentence** |
|---|---|---|
| **Person** | Tom broke the window. | **It was Tom who broke the window.** |
| **Thing** | Tom bought a laptop. | **It was a laptop that Tom bought.** |
| **Place** | We met at the station. | **It was at the station that we met.** |
| **Time** | She arrived yesterday. | **It was yesterday that she arrived.** |
| **Reason** | He left because he was tired. | **It was because he was tired that he left.** |
| **Action/idea** | I need more time. | **What I need is more time.** |

---

# 9. Cleft Sentences vs Normal Sentences

| **Normal Sentence** | **Cleft Sentence** | **Emphasis** |
|---|---|---|
| John helped me. | **It was John who helped me.** | John |
| Mary bought the book. | **It was the book that Mary bought.** | The book |
| We met in Paris. | **It was in Paris that we met.** | Paris |
| She arrived on Monday. | **It was on Monday that she arrived.** | Monday |
| I need a holiday. | **What I need is a holiday.** | A holiday |
| He wants an apology. | **What he wants is an apology.** | An apology |

---

# 10. Why Use Cleft Sentences?

| **Purpose** | **Example** |
|---|---|
| **Emphasize a person** | **It was Anna who solved the problem.** |
| **Emphasize a thing** | **It was the blue one that I wanted.** |
| **Emphasize a place** | **It was in London that they met.** |
| **Emphasize a time** | **It was last year that we moved.** |
| **Emphasize a reason** | **It was because of the rain that we stayed home.** |
| **Emphasize an idea/action** | **What I want is a better solution.** |
| **Correct information** | **It wasn't John who called; it was Peter.** |

---

# 11. Quick Summary

| **Cleft Type** | **Key Structure** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| **It-cleft** | It + be + X + who/that... | **It was John who called.** |
| **Wh-cleft** | What + clause + be + X | **What I need is money.** |
| **All-cleft** | All + clause + be + X | **All I need is rest.** |
| **Reverse wh-cleft** | X + be + what-clause | **Money is what I need.** |

---

## ⭐ Key Point

> **Cleft sentences are mainly used to emphasize specific information.**

### Easy Pattern 1: It-Cleft

> **It + is/was + emphasized information + who/that + clause**

**It was Sarah who called me.**

### Easy Pattern 2: Wh-Cleft

> **What + clause + is/was + emphasized information**

**What I need is more time.**

### Compare

| **Normal** | **Emphatic / Cleft** |
|---|---|
| John broke the window. | **It was John who broke the window.** |
| I need more time. | **What I need is more time.** |
| We met in London. | **It was in London that we met.** |

**Remember: Cleft sentence = split the sentence to put special focus on one part.**
