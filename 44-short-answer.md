# Summary: Short Answers in English

## 1. What Is a Short Answer?

A **short answer** is a brief response to a **yes/no question**. It normally uses an **auxiliary verb or modal verb** instead of repeating the entire sentence.

### Example

**Question:**

> Are you ready?

**Short answer:**

> Yes, **I am**.

Instead of:

> Yes, I am ready.

---

# 2. Basic Structure

| **Question** | **Positive Short Answer** | **Negative Short Answer** |
|---|---|---|
| Are you ready? | Yes, **I am**. | No, **I'm not**. |
| Is she happy? | Yes, **she is**. | No, **she isn't**. |
| Do they work here? | Yes, **they do**. | No, **they don't**. |
| Does he like coffee? | Yes, **he does**. | No, **he doesn't**. |
| Did you call her? | Yes, **I did**. | No, **I didn't**. |
| Have they arrived? | Yes, **they have**. | No, **they haven't**. |
| Can she swim? | Yes, **she can**. | No, **she can't**. |
| Will you come? | Yes, **I will**. | No, **I won't**. |

---

# 3. Short Answers with "Be"

Use **am, is, or are** in the short answer.

| **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|
| Are you tired? | Yes, **I am**. | No, **I'm not**. |
| Is he a teacher? | Yes, **he is**. | No, **he isn't**. |
| Is she at home? | Yes, **she is**. | No, **she isn't**. |
| Are they ready? | Yes, **they are**. | No, **they aren't**. |
| Are we late? | Yes, **we are**. | No, **we aren't**. |

### Example

> **Q:** Is she your sister?  
> **A:** Yes, **she is**.

---

# 4. Short Answers with Do / Does

Use **do** or **does** for the present simple.

| **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|
| Do you like coffee? | Yes, **I do**. | No, **I don't**. |
| Do they live here? | Yes, **they do**. | No, **they don't**. |
| Does he speak English? | Yes, **he does**. | No, **he doesn't**. |
| Does she work here? | Yes, **she does**. | No, **she doesn't**. |

### Example

> **Q:** Does he play football?  
> **A:** Yes, **he does**.

---

# 5. Short Answers with Did

Use **did** for the past simple.

| **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|
| Did you call me? | Yes, **I did**. | No, **I didn't**. |
| Did she arrive early? | Yes, **she did**. | No, **she didn't**. |
| Did they finish the work? | Yes, **they did**. | No, **they didn't**. |
| Did he buy the book? | Yes, **he did**. | No, **he didn't**. |

### Example

> **Q:** Did you see John?  
> **A:** No, **I didn't**.

---

# 6. Short Answers with Have / Has / Had

Use **have, has, or had** when they are auxiliary verbs.

| **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|
| Have you finished? | Yes, **I have**. | No, **I haven't**. |
| Has she arrived? | Yes, **she has**. | No, **she hasn't**. |
| Have they eaten? | Yes, **they have**. | No, **they haven't**. |
| Had he left? | Yes, **he had**. | No, **he hadn't**. |

### Example

> **Q:** Have you finished your homework?  
> **A:** Yes, **I have**.

---

# 7. Short Answers with Modal Verbs

Use the same modal verb from the question.

| **Modal** | **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **can** | Can you swim? | Yes, **I can**. | No, **I can't**. |
| **could** | Could she help? | Yes, **she could**. | No, **she couldn't**. |
| **will** | Will they come? | Yes, **they will**. | No, **they won't**. |
| **would** | Would he agree? | Yes, **he would**. | No, **he wouldn't**. |
| **should** | Should I go? | Yes, **you should**. | No, **you shouldn't**. |
| **must** | Must we leave? | Yes, **you must**. | No, **you needn't**. |
| **may** | May I come in? | Yes, **you may**. | No, **you may not**. |

---

# 8. Short Answers with "There"

For questions beginning with **Is there...?** or **Are there...?**, use **there** in the answer.

| **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|
| Is there a problem? | Yes, **there is**. | No, **there isn't**. |
| Is there any milk? | Yes, **there is**. | No, **there isn't**. |
| Are there any students? | Yes, **there are**. | No, **there aren't**. |
| Are there enough chairs? | Yes, **there are**. | No, **there aren't**. |

### Example

> **Q:** Is there a bank nearby?  
> **A:** Yes, **there is**.

---

# 9. Short Answers with "Have Got"

In British English, **have got** is common for possession.

| **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|
| Have you got a car? | Yes, **I have**. | No, **I haven't**. |
| Has she got a brother? | Yes, **she has**. | No, **she hasn't**. |
| Have they got enough money? | Yes, **they have**. | No, **they haven't**. |

### Example

> **Q:** Has he got a passport?  
> **A:** Yes, **he has**.

---

# 10. Short Answers with "Used To"

For questions with **used to**, use **did** in the short answer.

| **Question** | **Positive** | **Negative** |
|---|---|---|
| Did you use to live here? | Yes, **I did**. | No, **I didn't**. |
| Did she use to play tennis? | Yes, **she did**. | No, **she didn't**. |

### Example

> **Q:** Did you use to work here?  
> **A:** Yes, **I did**.

---

# 11. Subject-Verb Agreement

The subject in the short answer must match the question.

| **Question** | **Correct Answer** |
|---|---|
| Is **he** ready? | Yes, **he is**. |
| Is **she** ready? | Yes, **she is**. |
| Are **they** ready? | Yes, **they are**. |
| Does **John** work here? | Yes, **he does**. |
| Do **the students** understand? | Yes, **they do**. |

### 📌 Rule

> **Question subject → appropriate pronoun in the answer**

---

# 13. Short Answers vs. Full Answers

| **Question** | **Full Answer** | **Short Answer** |
|---|---|---|
| Are you tired? | Yes, I am tired. | Yes, **I am**. |
| Do you like tea? | Yes, I like tea. | Yes, **I do**. |
| Did she call? | No, she didn't call. | No, **she didn't**. |
| Can he swim? | Yes, he can swim. | Yes, **he can**. |
| Have they arrived? | No, they haven't arrived. | No, **they haven't**. |

### ⭐ Why Use Short Answers?

They are:

- **Natural**
- **Concise**
- **Less repetitive**
- Very common in **conversation**

---

# 14. Common Mistakes

| **Incorrect** | **Correct** | **Reason** |
|---|---|---|
| ❌ Yes, I am like coffee. | ✅ Yes, **I do**. | Use **do** with *like*. |
| ❌ Yes, I like. | ✅ Yes, **I do**. | Use the auxiliary **do**. |
| ❌ Yes, she does happy. | ✅ Yes, **she is**. | Use **be** with *happy*. |
| ❌ No, he isn't like it. | ✅ No, **he doesn't**. | Use **doesn't** with *like*. |
| ❌ Yes, they can swim. | ✅ Yes, **they can**. | Short answers use the modal. |

---

# 15. Quick Summary

| **Question Type** | **Use in Short Answer** | **Example** |
|---|---|---|
| **Be** | am/is/are/was/were | Are you ready? → Yes, **I am**. |
| **Present simple** | do/does | Do you work here? → Yes, **I do**. |
| **Past simple** | did | Did she call? → No, **she didn't**. |
| **Present perfect** | have/has | Have you finished? → Yes, **I have**. |
| **Past perfect** | had | Had he left? → Yes, **he had**. |
| **Modal** | Same modal | Can you swim? → Yes, **I can**. |
| **There is/are** | there is/are | Is there a problem? → No, **there isn't**. |
| **Have got** | have/has | Have you got a car? → Yes, **I have**. |

---

## ⭐ Key Point

> **Short answer = Yes/No + subject + auxiliary/modal verb.**

### 💡 Easy Formula

**Question:**

> Do you like English?

**Short answer:**

> Yes, **I do**.  
> No, **I don't**.

### Remember

| **Question Uses** | **Answer Uses** |
|---|---|
| **am/is/are** | am/is/are |
| **do/does** | do/does |
| **did** | did |
| **have/has** | have/has |
| **can** | can |
| **will** | will |
| **should** | should |
| **would** | would |

> **Don't repeat the whole sentence — repeat the auxiliary or modal instead.**
