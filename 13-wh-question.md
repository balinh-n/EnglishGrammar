# WH-Questions in English

## 1. What Is a WH-Question?

A **WH-question** is a question that begins with a **WH-word** or a related question expression.

WH-questions are used to ask for **specific information**, rather than simply answering **yes** or **no**.

Common WH-words include:

- Who
- What
- When
- Where
- Why
- Which
- Whose
- Whom
- How

---

## 2. Summary of WH-Questions

| WH-Question | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Who?** | Asks about a person or people | **Who** is your teacher? |
| **What?** | Asks about a thing, action, or information | **What** are you doing? |
| **When?** | Asks about time or a date | **When** is your birthday? |
| **Where?** | Asks about a place or location | **Where** do you live? |
| **Why?** | Asks about a reason | **Why** are you late? |
| **Which?** | Asks about a choice | **Which** book do you want? |
| **Whose?** | Asks about ownership | **Whose** bag is this? |
| **Whom?** | Asks about a person receiving an action | **Whom** did you meet? |
| **How?** | Asks about manner or method | **How** do you go to school? |
| **How many?** | Asks about a number of countable things | **How many** books do you have? |
| **How much?** | Asks about an amount or price | **How much** is this? |
| **How often?** | Asks about frequency | **How often** do you exercise? |
| **How long?** | Asks about duration or length | **How long** have you lived here? |
| **How far?** | Asks about distance | **How far** is the school? |
| **How old?** | Asks about age | **How old** are you? |

---

# 3. Who

**Who** is used to ask about a **person or people**.

### Examples

> **Who** is that man?

> **Who** is your best friend?

> **Who** called you?

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Ask about a person | **Who** is she? |
| Ask about the subject | **Who** broke the window? |
| Ask about people | **Who** are they? |

---

# 4. What

**What** is used to ask about a **thing, action, information, or idea**.

### Examples

> **What** is your name?

> **What** are you doing?

> **What** do you want?

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Thing | **What** is this? |
| Action | **What** are you doing? |
| Information | **What** is your address? |
| Choice in a general sense | **What** do you want to eat? |

---

# 5. When

**When** is used to ask about **time or a date**.

### Examples

> **When** is your birthday?

> **When** do you go to work?

> **When** did the movie start?

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Time | **When** do you wake up? |
| Date | **When** is the meeting? |
| Past event | **When** did you arrive? |
| Future event | **When** will you come back? |

---

# 6. Where

**Where** is used to ask about a **place or location**.

### Examples

> **Where** do you live?

> **Where** is my phone?

> **Where** did you go yesterday?

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Location | **Where** is the bank? |
| Home | **Where** do you live? |
| Destination | **Where** are you going? |
| Past location | **Where** did you stay? |

---

# 7. Why

**Why** is used to ask about a **reason or cause**.

### Examples

> **Why** are you crying?

> **Why** did you leave early?

> **Why** are you learning English?

A common answer to **why** begins with:

> **Because...**

**Example:**

> Why are you tired?

> **Because** I didn't sleep well.

---

# 8. Which

**Which** is used when choosing between **two or more specific people or things**.

### Examples

> **Which** color do you prefer?

> **Which** shirt is yours?

> **Which** bus should I take?

### Which vs. What

| Question | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | General information or an open choice | **What** do you want to eat? |
| **Which** | A choice from a limited or specific group | **Which** dessert do you want: cake or ice cream? |

---

# 9. Whose

**Whose** is used to ask about **ownership or possession**.

### Examples

> **Whose** book is this?

> **Whose** phone is ringing?

> **Whose** car did you use?

### Common Structure

> Whose + noun + ...?

**Example:**

> **Whose bag** is this?

It can also be used without a noun:

> **Whose is this?**

---

# 10. Whom

**Whom** is used to ask about a person who is the **object of a verb or preposition**.

It is more common in **formal English**.

### Examples

> **Whom** did you meet?

> **Whom** did she invite?

> **Whom** are you talking to?

In everyday English, **who** is often used instead of **whom**.

> Who did you meet?

This is very common and natural in spoken English.

---

# 11. How

**How** is used to ask about **the way, method, condition, or manner**.

### Examples

> **How** are you?

> **How** do you cook this?

> **How** did you solve the problem?

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Manner | **How** did you do it? |
| Condition | **How** are you? |
| Method | **How** do you make coffee? |
| Means | **How** do you go to work? |

---

# 12. How Many

**How many** is used to ask about the **number of countable nouns**.

### Examples

> **How many** students are in your class?

> **How many** books do you have?

> **How many** apples did you buy?

### Structure

> How many + plural countable noun + ...?

**Example:**

> How many **cars** do you have?

---

# 13. How Much

**How much** is used to ask about an **amount of an uncountable noun** or a **price**.

### Examples

> **How much** water do you drink?

> **How much** money do you need?

> **How much** is this shirt?

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Uncountable amount | **How much** water do you need? |
| Money | **How much** money do you have? |
| Price | **How much** is this? |

---

# 14. How Often

**How often** is used to ask about **frequency**.

### Examples

> **How often** do you exercise?

> **How often** do you visit your parents?

> **How often** does she go shopping?

Common answers include:

- Always
- Usually
- Often
- Sometimes
- Rarely
- Never
- Once a week
- Twice a month

**Example:**

> How often do you exercise?

> I exercise **three times a week**.

---

# 15. How Long

**How long** is used to ask about **duration or length**.

### Examples

> **How long** is the movie?

> **How long** have you lived here?

> **How long** does the journey take?

| Meaning | Example |
|---|---|
| Duration | **How long** did you wait? |
| Length | **How long** is the table? |
| Period of time | **How long** have you studied English? |

---

# 16. How Far

**How far** is used to ask about **distance**.

### Examples

> **How far** is the airport?

> **How far** is your school from your house?

> **How far** did you walk?

**Example answer:**

> How far is the station?

> It is **about 5 kilometers** away.

---

# 17. How Old

**How old** is used to ask about **age**.

### Examples

> **How old** are you?

> **How old** is your brother?

> **How old** is this building?

**Example:**

> How old are you?

> I am **twenty years old**.

---

# 18. Basic WH-Question Structure

A common WH-question structure is:

> **WH-word + auxiliary verb + subject + main verb?**

### Examples

> **What do you eat?**

> **Where does she live?**

> **When did they arrive?**

> **Why are you crying?**

| WH-word | Auxiliary | Subject | Main Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | do | you | eat? |
| Where | does | she | live? |
| When | did | they | arrive? |
| Why | are | you | crying? |

---

# 19. WH-Questions with "Be"

When the main verb is **be**, the structure is usually:

> **WH-word + be + subject?**

### Examples

> **Who is** he?

> **What is** this?

> **Where are** they?

> **Why are** you angry?

| Question | Example Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who is he?** | He is my brother. |
| **What is this?** | It is a book. |
| **Where are they?** | They are at school. |
| **Why are you tired?** | Because I worked late. |

---

# 20. WH-Questions with Modal Verbs

WH-questions can also use modal verbs such as:

> can, could, should, would, may, might, must

### Examples

> **What can** I do?

> **Where should** we go?

> **When can** you come?

> **What should** I bring?

### Structure

> **WH-word + modal + subject + base verb?**

**Example:**

> What **should** I **do**?

---

# 21. WH-Questions About the Subject

Sometimes the WH-word itself is the **subject** of the sentence.

In this case, we usually **do not use do/does/did**.

### Examples

> **Who called** you?

> **Who broke** the window?

> **What happened?**

Compare:

| Subject Question | Object Question |
|---|---|
| **Who called you?** | **Who did you call?** |
| **Who broke the window?** | **Who did you see?** |
| **What happened?** | **What did you buy?** |

---

# 22. WH-Questions in One Sentence

Consider this sentence:

> **Sarah went to the library on Monday because she needed a book.**

We can ask different WH-questions:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who** went to the library? | Sarah. |
| **Where** did Sarah go? | To the library. |
| **When** did Sarah go? | On Monday. |
| **Why** did Sarah go there? | Because she needed a book. |
| **What** did she need? | A book. |

## Key Point

WH-questions are used when we need **specific information**.

A simple way to remember them is:

> **Who = person**  
> **What = thing**  
> **When = time**  
> **Where = place**  
> **Why = reason**  
> **Which = choice**  
> **Whose = possession**  
> **How = manner**  
> **How many = number**  
> **How much = amount / price**
