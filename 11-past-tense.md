# Past Tense Summary

## 1. Past Tenses Overview

| Tense | Structure | Main Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Past Simple** | S + V2 | Completed past actions, past habits, past facts | She **worked** yesterday. |
| **Past Continuous** | S + was/were + V-ing | Actions in progress at a past time | She **was working** at 8 p.m. |
| **Past Perfect** | S + had + V3 | Action completed before another past action | She **had finished** before I arrived. |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | S + had been + V-ing | Duration before a past point | She **had been working** for two hours. |

---

## 2. Past Simple

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | S + V2 | She **worked** yesterday. |
| **Negative** | S + did not + V1 | She **didn't work** yesterday. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Did + S + V1? | **Did** she **work** yesterday? |
| **WH-Question** | WH + did + S + V1? | Where **did** she **work**? |

### Uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Completed past action | I **visited** Paris last year. |
| Action at a specific past time | She **called** me yesterday. |
| Series of past actions | I **woke up**, **ate** breakfast, and **went** to work. |
| Past habit | We **played** football every Sunday. |
| Past fact | He **lived** in London in 2010. |
| Past situation | She **worked** at a bank for five years. |

### Common Signal Words

| Signal Words |
|---|
| yesterday |
| last night |
| last week |
| last month |
| last year |
| ago |
| in 2010 |
| when I was young |
| then |
| once |

### Important Rule

After **did/didn't**, use **V1**.

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| Did you **went** there? | Did you **go** there? |
| She didn't **went** home. | She didn't **go** home. |

---

## 3. Past Continuous

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | S + was/were + V-ing | She **was working**. |
| **Negative** | S + was/were not + V-ing | She **wasn't working**. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Was/Were + S + V-ing? | **Was** she **working**? |
| **WH-Question** | WH + was/were + S + V-ing? | What **was** she **doing**? |

### Uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Action in progress at a past time | I **was studying** at 8 p.m. |
| Interrupted action | I **was sleeping** when he called. |
| Two actions happening at the same time | She **was cooking** while I **was cleaning**. |
| Background action | The sun **was shining** and birds **were singing**. |
| Temporary past situation | He **was living** in London that year. |
| Action happening around a particular time | They **were traveling** around Asia that month. |

### Common Signal Words

| Signal Words |
|---|
| while |
| when |
| at that moment |
| at 8 p.m. |
| all evening |
| all morning |
| during that time |

### Common Pattern

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Past Continuous + when + Past Simple | I **was sleeping when** he **called**. |
| Past Simple + while + Past Continuous | He **called while** I **was sleeping**. |
| Past Continuous + while + Past Continuous | I **was cooking while** she **was cleaning**. |

---

## 4. Past Perfect

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | S + had + V3 | She **had finished**. |
| **Negative** | S + had not + V3 | She **hadn't finished**. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Had + S + V3? | **Had** she **finished**? |
| **WH-Question** | WH + had + S + V3? | What **had** she **done**? |

### Main Use

The Past Perfect describes an action that happened **before another past action or past time**.

### Timeline

| Earlier Past | Later Past |
|---|---|
| She **had left**. | I **arrived**. |

Example:

> She **had left** before I **arrived**.

### Uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Earlier past action | She **had left** before I arrived. |
| Action completed before a past time | They **had finished** by 6 p.m. |
| Cause of a past situation | He was tired because he **had worked** all day. |
| Past experience before another past event | She **had never seen** snow before she moved there. |

### Common Signal Words

| Signal Words |
|---|
| before |
| after |
| already |
| just |
| never |
| by the time |
| until then |
| previously |

## 5. Past Perfect Continuous

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | S + had been + V-ing | She **had been working**. |
| **Negative** | S + had not been + V-ing | She **hadn't been working**. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Had + S + been + V-ing? | **Had** she **been working**? |
| **WH-Question** | How long + had + S + been + V-ing? | How long **had** she **been working**? |

### Uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Duration before a past point | I **had been studying** for three hours before dinner. |
| Activity continuing before another past action | They **had been waiting** before the bus arrived. |
| Cause of a past result | She was tired because she **had been working** all day. |
| Emphasis on duration | He **had been living** there for ten years before he moved. |

### Common Signal Words

| Signal Words |
|---|
| for |
| since |
| before |
| until |
| all day |
| all morning |
| how long |

## 6. Past Tenses Comparison

| Tense | Main Idea | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Past Simple** | Finished past action | I **worked** yesterday. |
| **Past Continuous** | Action in progress in the past | I **was working** at 8 p.m. |
| **Past Perfect** | Earlier completed past action | I **had worked** before he arrived. |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | Duration before a past point | I **had been working** for two hours before he arrived. |

---

## 7. Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

| Past Simple | Past Continuous |
|---|---|
| Completed action | Action in progress |
| Main event | Background action |
| Short event | Longer ongoing action |
| He **called** me. | I **was sleeping**. |
| She **opened** the door. | I **was watching** TV. |

### Example

> I **was sleeping when** he **called**.

The **Past Continuous** shows the action already in progress.

The **Past Simple** shows the event that happened during or interrupted it.

---

## 8. Past Simple vs. Past Perfect

| Past Simple | Past Perfect |
|---|---|
| Later past action | Earlier past action |
| I **arrived**. | She **had left**. |
| He **called**. | I **had gone** home. |

### Example

> When I **arrived**, she **had already left**.

### Timeline

| Order | Action |
|---|---|
| 1st | She **had left**. |
| 2nd | I **arrived**. |

---

## 9. Past Perfect vs. Past Perfect Continuous

| Past Perfect | Past Perfect Continuous |
|---|---|
| Focus on completed result | Focus on duration/activity |
| had + V3 | had been + V-ing |
| She **had written** five emails. | She **had been writing** emails for two hours. |
| He **had cleaned** the room. | He **had been cleaning** the room all morning. |

---

## 10. Past Simple vs. Past Perfect vs. Past Perfect Continuous

| Tense | Question to Ask | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Past Simple | What happened? | I **worked** yesterday. |
| Past Perfect | What had happened first? | I **had worked** before he arrived. |
| Past Perfect Continuous | How long had it been happening? | I **had been working** for two hours before he arrived. |

## 17. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Did you **went** there? | Did you **go** there? | After did → V1 |
| She didn't **went** home. | She didn't **go** home. | After didn't → V1 |
| I have no relation to past tense here. | I **had gone** home before he arrived. | Earlier past → Past Perfect |
| I was **sleep** when he called. | I was **sleeping** when he called. | was/were + V-ing |
| She had **went** home. | She had **gone** home. | had + V3 |
| He had been **work** all day. | He had been **working** all day. | had been + V-ing |
| When I arrived, she **left already**. | When I arrived, she **had already left**. | Earlier action → Past Perfect |
| I was knowing the answer. | I **knew** the answer. | Stative verb normally uses simple form |
