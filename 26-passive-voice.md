# Passive Voice in English

## 1. What Is Passive Voice?

The **passive voice** is used when the **action or the receiver of the action** is more important than the person who performs it.

### Active Voice

> The chef **cooked the meal**.

### Passive Voice

> The meal **was cooked by the chef**.

| Voice | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Subject + verb + object | The chef cooked the meal. |
| **Passive** | Object + be + past participle (+ by + subject) | The meal was cooked by the chef. |

---

# 2. Basic Structure of Passive Voice

### General Pattern

> **Subject + be + past participle (V3)**

The original object becomes the subject of the passive sentence.

### Example

Active:

> The students **cleaned** the classroom.

Passive:

> The classroom **was cleaned** by the students.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| The students | The classroom |
| cleaned | was cleaned |
| the classroom | by the students |

---

# 3. Passive Voice in Different Tenses

| Tense | Active Example | Passive Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | They clean the room. | The room **is cleaned** by them. |
| **Present Continuous** | They are cleaning the room. | The room **is being cleaned** by them. |
| **Present Perfect** | They have cleaned the room. | The room **has been cleaned** by them. |
| **Past Simple** | They cleaned the room. | The room **was cleaned** by them. |
| **Past Continuous** | They were cleaning the room. | The room **was being cleaned** by them. |
| **Past Perfect** | They had cleaned the room. | The room **had been cleaned** by them. |
| **Future Simple** | They will clean the room. | The room **will be cleaned** by them. |
| **Future Perfect** | They will have cleaned the room. | The room **will have been cleaned** by them. |
| **Modal** | They can clean the room. | The room **can be cleaned** by them. |

---

# 4. Present Simple Passive

### Structure

> **am / is / are + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: They **make** cars here.

> Passive: Cars **are made** here.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| People speak English here. | English **is spoken** here. |
| They sell computers online. | Computers **are sold** online. |
| They clean the rooms every day. | The rooms **are cleaned** every day. |

---

# 5. Present Continuous Passive

### Structure

> **am / is / are + being + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: They **are building** a new school.

> Passive: A new school **is being built**.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| They are repairing the road. | The road **is being repaired**. |
| They are painting the house. | The house **is being painted**. |
| They are preparing the food. | The food **is being prepared**. |

---

# 6. Present Perfect Passive

### Structure

> **has / have + been + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: They **have finished** the work.

> Passive: The work **has been finished**.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| They have completed the project. | The project **has been completed**. |
| They have repaired the car. | The car **has been repaired**. |
| They have cleaned the room. | The room **has been cleaned**. |

---

# 7. Past Simple Passive

### Structure

> **was / were + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: They **built** the house in 2020.

> Passive: The house **was built** in 2020.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| They opened the store. | The store **was opened**. |
| Someone stole my phone. | My phone **was stolen**. |
| They repaired the road. | The road **was repaired**. |

---

# 8. Past Continuous Passive

### Structure

> **was / were + being + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: They **were repairing** the bridge.

> Passive: The bridge **was being repaired**.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| They were painting the house. | The house **was being painted**. |
| They were cleaning the room. | The room **was being cleaned**. |
| They were repairing the machine. | The machine **was being repaired**. |

---

# 9. Past Perfect Passive

### Structure

> **had + been + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: They **had completed** the work.

> Passive: The work **had been completed**.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| They had finished the project. | The project **had been finished**. |
| They had cleaned the room. | The room **had been cleaned**. |
| They had repaired the car. | The car **had been repaired**. |

---

# 10. Future Simple Passive

### Structure

> **will + be + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: They **will build** a new hospital.

> Passive: A new hospital **will be built**.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| They will announce the results tomorrow. | The results **will be announced** tomorrow. |
| They will finish the work soon. | The work **will be finished** soon. |
| They will deliver the package. | The package **will be delivered**. |

---

# 11. Future Perfect Passive

### Structure

> **will have + been + past participle**

### Example

> Active: They **will have completed** the project by Friday.

> Passive: The project **will have been completed** by Friday.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| They will have finished the work. | The work **will have been finished**. |
| They will have completed the building. | The building **will have been completed**. |

---

# 12. Passive Voice with Modal Verbs

Modal verbs include:

> can, could, may, might, must, should, would

### Structure

> **modal + be + past participle**

### Examples

> Active: You **must finish** the work.

> Passive: The work **must be finished**.

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| You must obey the rules. | The rules **must be obeyed**. |
| They can solve the problem. | The problem **can be solved**. |
| We should clean the room. | The room **should be cleaned**. |
| They may cancel the meeting. | The meeting **may be canceled**. |

---

# 13. Passive Voice with "By"

**By + agent** tells us who performed the action.

### Example

> The novel was written **by George Orwell**.

> The window was broken **by the boy**.

| Passive Sentence | Agent |
|---|---|
| The book was written **by the author**. | the author |
| The car was repaired **by the mechanic**. | the mechanic |
| The cake was made **by my mother**. | my mother |

### Important

The **by-phrase** can often be omitted when the person is unknown, obvious, or unimportant.

> The window was broken.

Instead of:

> The window was broken **by someone**.

---

# 14. When Do We Use Passive Voice?

| Use | Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Agent is unknown** | We don't know who did it | My phone **was stolen**. |
| **Agent is unimportant** | The action is more important | The road **was repaired**. |
| **Agent is obvious** | Everyone knows who did it | The criminal **was arrested**. |
| **Formal writing** | Common in reports and academic writing | The experiment **was conducted** carefully. |
| **Focus on result** | The result is more important | The project **has been completed**. |
| **Scientific writing** | Focuses on processes | The samples **were tested**. |

---

# 15. Active to Passive: Basic Steps

### Active Sentence

> The teacher **explained** the lesson.

### Step 1: Find the object

> the lesson

### Step 2: Move the object to the beginning

> The lesson...

### Step 3: Change the verb to the correct form of **be + V3**

> The lesson **was explained**...

### Step 4: Add the original subject with "by" if necessary

> The lesson **was explained by the teacher**.

| Step | Action | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find the object | the lesson |
| 2 | Move it to the beginning | The lesson... |
| 3 | Use **be + V3** | was explained |
| 4 | Add **by + agent** | by the teacher |

# 16. Active vs. Passive

| Active Voice | Passive Voice |
|---|---|
| The chef cooked the meal. | The meal was cooked by the chef. |
| They built the bridge. | The bridge was built by them. |
| Someone stole my bike. | My bike was stolen. |
| They are repairing the road. | The road is being repaired. |
| They have completed the project. | The project has been completed. |
| They will announce the results. | The results will be announced. |

---

# 17. Passive Voice with Two Objects

Some verbs can have **two objects**, such as:

> give, send, offer, tell, show, teach, lend

### Active

> She **gave me a book**.

Two passive forms are possible:

> **I was given a book.**

> **A book was given to me.**

| Active | Passive 1 | Passive 2 |
|---|---|---|
| She gave me a book. | I was given a book. | A book was given to me. |
| They sent me an email. | I was sent an email. | An email was sent to me. |
| He offered her a job. | She was offered a job. | A job was offered to her. |

---

# 18. Passive Voice with Infinitives

### Structure

> **to be + past participle**

### Examples

> The work needs **to be finished**.

> The room needs **to be cleaned**.

> The problem needs **to be solved**.

| Active Idea | Passive Infinitive |
|---|---|
| finish the work | the work needs **to be finished** |
| clean the room | the room needs **to be cleaned** |
| solve the problem | the problem needs **to be solved** |

---

# 19. Passive Voice with Gerunds

### Structure

> **being + past participle**

### Examples

> I don't like **being criticized**.

> She hates **being ignored**.

> He enjoys **being invited** to parties.

| Verb | Passive Gerund |
|---|---|
| criticize | **being criticized** |
| ignore | **being ignored** |
| invite | **being invited** |
| treat | **being treated** |

---

# 20. Passive Questions

The passive voice can also be used in questions.

### Examples

> **Was the car repaired?**

> **Is English spoken here?**

> **Has the work been completed?**

> **When was the building constructed?**

| Active Question | Passive Question |
|---|---|
| Did they repair the car? | **Was the car repaired?** |
| Do they speak English here? | **Is English spoken here?** |
| Have they finished the work? | **Has the work been finished?** |
| When did they build it? | **When was it built?** |

---

# 21. Passive Negatives

Put **not** after the auxiliary verb.

### Examples

> The work **was not finished**.

> The car **is not being repaired**.

> The project **has not been completed**.

> The results **will not be announced** today.

| Tense | Passive Negative |
|---|---|
| Present Simple | is **not made** |
| Past Simple | was **not made** |
| Present Continuous | is **not being made** |
| Present Perfect | has **not been made** |
| Future | will **not be made** |

---

# 22. Get-Passive

Informally, **get + past participle** can sometimes be used instead of **be + past participle**.

### Examples

> He **got injured** in the accident.

> She **got promoted** last year.

> My phone **got stolen**.

| Be-Passive | Get-Passive |
|---|---|
| He was injured. | He got injured. |
| She was promoted. | She got promoted. |
| My phone was stolen. | My phone got stolen. |

**Get-passive** is more common in informal English.

---

# 23. Stative Passive

Sometimes **be + past participle** describes a **state**, rather than an action.

### Examples

> The door **is closed**.

> The window **is broken**.

> The shop **is closed**.

Compare:

> Someone **closed** the door. → Action

> The door **is closed**. → State

| Action | State |
|---|---|
| Someone closed the door. | The door is closed. |
| Someone broke the window. | The window is broken. |
| Someone opened the shop. | The shop is open. |

---

# 24. Verbs That Cannot Normally Become Passive

A passive sentence normally requires a verb with an **object**.

Transitive:

> They **built the house**.  
> → The house was built.

Intransitive:

> He **arrived**.  
> → No direct object, so a normal passive form is not possible.

| Verb Type | Example | Passive? |
|---|---|---|
| **Transitive** | They built the house. | Yes |
| **Transitive** | She opened the door. | Yes |
| **Intransitive** | He arrived. | No |
| **Intransitive** | They slept. | No |
| **Intransitive** | The baby cried. | No |

---

# 25. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| The car **was repair**. | The car **was repaired**. | Use the past participle (V3). |
| The work **is finish**. | The work **is finished**. | Use **be + V3**. |
| The house **was built by they**. | The house **was built by them**. | Use an object pronoun after **by**. |
| The work **will finished** tomorrow. | The work **will be finished** tomorrow. | Future passive = **will be + V3**. |
| The road **is repairing**. | The road **is being repaired**. | Present continuous passive = **is being + V3**. |
| The project **has completed**. | The project **has been completed**. | Present perfect passive = **has been + V3**. |

# 27. How to Identify Passive Voice

Look for:

> **Form of "be" + past participle (V3)**

### Examples

| Sentence | Passive? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| The car **was repaired**. | Yes | was + repaired |
| The car **is being repaired**. | Yes | is + being + repaired |
| The car **has been repaired**. | Yes | has + been + repaired |
| They **repaired** the car. | No | Active voice |
| She **is repairing** the car. | No | Active continuous |

# 29. Quick Summary

| Feature | Passive Voice |
|---|---|
| **Main focus** | The receiver of the action |
| **Basic structure** | Subject + be + V3 |
| **Optional agent** | by + person/thing |
| **Present Simple** | is/are + V3 |
| **Past Simple** | was/were + V3 |
| **Present Perfect** | has/have been + V3 |
| **Future** | will be + V3 |
| **Modal** | modal + be + V3 |

# 30. Easy Way to Remember

| Active Voice | Passive Voice |
|---|---|
| **Who does the action?** | **Who/what receives the action?** |
| The chef cooked the meal. | The meal was cooked by the chef. |
| Subject performs action. | Subject receives action. |
| Subject + Verb + Object | Object + be + V3 |

## Key Point

The **passive voice** focuses on the **receiver of an action** rather than the person who performs it.

### Main Pattern

> **Active:** Subject + Verb + Object

> The teacher **explained** the lesson.

> **Passive:** Object + be + Past Participle (+ by + Subject)

> The lesson **was explained** by the teacher.

### Remember

> **Passive = BE + V3**

The form of **BE** changes according to the tense:

> is **made**  
> was **made**  
> is being **made**  
> has been **made**  
> will be **made**  
> must be **made**
