# Reported Speech in English

## 1. What Is Reported Speech?

**Reported speech** is used to tell someone what another person **said, asked, or told** without using their exact words.

### Direct Speech

> Tom said, **"I am tired."**

### Reported Speech

> Tom said **that he was tired**.

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct speech** | Exact words of the speaker | She said, "I am busy." |
| **Reported speech** | Reports the speaker's words | She said that she was busy. |

---

# 2. Basic Changes in Reported Speech

When changing direct speech into reported speech, we may need to change:

- **Pronouns**
- **Tenses**
- **Time expressions**
- **Place expressions**
- **Demonstratives**
- **Word order in questions**

### Example

Direct:

> John said, "I will meet you here tomorrow."

Reported:

> John said that **he would meet me there the next day**.

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---|---|
| I | he |
| will | would |
| you | me |
| here | there |
| tomorrow | the next day |

---

# 3. Tense Changes

When the reporting verb is in the **past**, the tense often moves one step back.

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | Past Simple | "I work here." → He said that he **worked** there. |
| **Present Continuous** | Past Continuous | "I am working." → He said that he **was working**. |
| **Present Perfect** | Past Perfect | "I have finished." → He said that he **had finished**. |
| **Past Simple** | Past Perfect | "I saw her." → He said that he **had seen** her. |
| **Past Continuous** | Past Perfect Continuous | "I was working." → He said that he **had been working**. |
| **Will** | Would | "I will help." → He said that he **would help**. |
| **Can** | Could | "I can swim." → He said that he **could swim**. |
| **May** | Might | "I may go." → He said that he **might go**. |
| **Must** | Had to | "I must leave." → He said that he **had to leave**. |

---

# 4. Present Simple → Past Simple

### Direct

> She said, **"I like coffee."**

### Reported

> She said that she **liked coffee**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I live in Hanoi." | He said that he **lived in Hanoi**. |
| "I work at a bank." | She said that she **worked at a bank**. |
| "I like this book." | He said that he **liked that book**. |

---

# 5. Present Continuous → Past Continuous

### Direct

> She said, **"I am studying."**

### Reported

> She said that she **was studying**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I am working." | He said that he **was working**. |
| "We are waiting." | They said that they **were waiting**. |
| "She is sleeping." | He said that she **was sleeping**. |

---

# 6. Present Perfect → Past Perfect

### Direct

> He said, **"I have finished my work."**

### Reported

> He said that he **had finished his work**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I have finished." | She said that she **had finished**. |
| "We have seen the movie." | They said that they **had seen** the movie. |
| "I have lost my keys." | He said that he **had lost** his keys. |

---

# 7. Past Simple → Past Perfect

### Direct

> She said, **"I visited Paris."**

### Reported

> She said that she **had visited Paris**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I bought a car." | He said that he **had bought** a car. |
| "I saw John." | She said that she **had seen** John. |
| "We went home." | They said that they **had gone** home. |

---

# 8. Will → Would

### Direct

> He said, **"I will call you."**

### Reported

> He said that he **would call me**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| will | would |
| "I will help you." | He said that he **would help me**. |
| "We will come tomorrow." | They said that they **would come the next day**. |

---

# 9. Modal Verb Changes

| Direct | Reported | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | could | "I can swim." → He said he **could swim**. |
| **may** | might | "I may go." → She said she **might go**. |
| **will** | would | "I will help." → He said he **would help**. |
| **shall** | should / would | "I shall return." → He said he **would return**. |
| **must** | had to | "I must leave." → He said he **had to leave**. |

### Modals That Often Do Not Change

**could, would, should, might, ought to** generally remain unchanged.

> "I could help."  
> → He said that he **could help**.

> "You should study."  
> → He said that I **should study**.

---

# 10. Pronoun Changes

Pronouns change according to the **speaker and listener**.

### Example

Direct:

> Sarah said, **"I love my job."**

Reported:

> Sarah said that **she loved her job**.

| Direct | Possible Reported Form |
|---|---|
| I | he / she |
| we | they |
| my | his / her |
| our | their |
| me | him / her |
| us | them |
| you | me / him / her / us / them |

### Example

> Tom said to Mary, **"I will help you."**

> Tom told Mary that **he would help her**.

---

# 11. Time Expression Changes

Time expressions often change when the reporting situation is different.

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---|---|
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| now | then |
| tonight | that night |
| this week | that week |
| last week | the previous week |
| next week | the following week |
| ago | before |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |

### Example

Direct:

> She said, **"I will see you tomorrow."**

Reported:

> She said that she would see me **the next day**.

---

# 12. Say vs. Tell

**Say** and **tell** are used differently.

| Verb | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **say** | say + something | He **said that** he was tired. |
| **say** | say + to + person | He **said to me** that he was tired. |
| **tell** | tell + person + something | He **told me that** he was tired. |

### Important

> ❌ He told that he was tired.

> ✅ He **told me that** he was tired.

> ✅ He **said that** he was tired.

---

# 13. Reported Statements

Reported statements usually use:

> **said + (that) + clause**

or

> **told + object + (that) + clause**

### Examples

Direct:

> "I am tired," she said.

Reported:

> She said **that she was tired**.

Direct:

> "I am busy," John told me.

Reported:

> John told me **that he was busy**.

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---|---|
| "I am happy." | She said that she was happy. |
| "I live in London." | He said that he lived in London. |
| "We have finished." | They said that they had finished. |

---

# 14. Reported Yes/No Questions

For **yes/no questions**, use:

> **asked + if/whether + statement word order**

### Direct

> He asked, **"Are you busy?"**

### Reported

> He asked **if I was busy**.

### Important

Reported questions use **statement word order**, not question word order.

> ❌ He asked if **was I busy**.

> ✅ He asked if **I was busy**.

| Direct Question | Reported Question |
|---|---|
| "Are you ready?" | He asked if I was ready. |
| "Do you like coffee?" | She asked whether I liked coffee. |
| "Did he call?" | He asked if he had called. |
| "Can you help me?" | She asked whether I could help her. |

---

# 15. Reported WH-Questions

For **WH-questions**, keep the question word:

> **what, where, when, why, who, how, which, whose**

### Direct

> She asked, **"Where do you live?"**

### Reported

> She asked **where I lived**.

### Examples

| Direct Question | Reported Question |
|---|---|
| "Where do you live?" | She asked where I lived. |
| "What are you doing?" | He asked what I was doing. |
| "When will you arrive?" | She asked when I would arrive. |
| "Why did you leave?" | He asked why I had left. |
| "Who is that man?" | She asked who that man was. |
| "How can I help you?" | He asked how he could help me. |

---

# 16. Reported Commands

Commands can be reported using:

> **told + object + to + verb**

### Direct

> The teacher said, **"Open your books."**

### Reported

> The teacher **told us to open our books**.

| Direct Command | Reported Command |
|---|---|
| "Sit down." | He told me **to sit down**. |
| "Open the door." | She told him **to open the door**. |
| "Be quiet." | The teacher told us **to be quiet**. |
| "Wait here." | He told me **to wait there**. |

---

# 17. Reported Negative Commands

Negative commands use:

> **told + object + not to + verb**

### Examples

Direct:

> He said, **"Don't touch it."**

Reported:

> He told me **not to touch it**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "Don't run." | She told me **not to run**. |
| "Don't open the door." | He told us **not to open the door**. |
| "Don't be late." | She told him **not to be late**. |

---

# 18. Reported Requests

Requests can use:

> **asked + object + to + verb**

### Direct

> She said, **"Please help me."**

### Reported

> She **asked me to help her**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "Please help me." | She asked me **to help her**. |
| "Please wait." | He asked me **to wait**. |
| "Please close the door." | She asked him **to close the door**. |

---

# 19. Reported Suggestions

Suggestions can be reported using several structures.

### "Let's..."

> "Let's go home."

→ He suggested **going home**.

### "Why don't we...?"

> "Why don't we take a taxi?"

→ She suggested **taking a taxi**.

### "We should..."

> "We should leave early."

→ He suggested **that we should leave early**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "Let's go." | He suggested **going**. |
| "Let's study together." | She suggested **studying together**. |
| "Why don't we leave?" | He suggested **leaving**. |
| "We should take a taxi." | She suggested **that we should take a taxi**. |

---

# 20. Reported Advice

Advice can be reported using:

> **advised + object + to + verb**

### Direct

> The doctor said, **"You should rest."**

### Reported

> The doctor **advised me to rest**.

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "You should study." | The teacher advised me **to study**. |
| "You should see a doctor." | She advised him **to see a doctor**. |
| "You shouldn't drive." | He advised me **not to drive**. |

---

# 21. Reporting Verbs

Different reporting verbs can express different meanings.

| Reporting Verb | Meaning / Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **say** | Report words | He said that he was tired. |
| **tell** | Give information to someone | She told me that she was busy. |
| **ask** | Report a question/request | He asked if I was ready. |
| **order** | Give a strong command | The officer ordered him to stop. |
| **advise** | Give advice | She advised me to wait. |
| **warn** | Give a warning | He warned me not to go. |
| **promise** | Make a promise | She promised to help me. |
| **offer** | Offer to do something | He offered to help. |
| **suggest** | Give an idea | She suggested going home. |
| **invite** | Ask someone to come | He invited me to dinner. |
| **refuse** | Say no to something | She refused to answer. |
| **agree** | Accept a suggestion/request | He agreed to help. |
| **deny** | Say something was not true | He denied stealing the money. |
| **admit** | Accept that something is true | She admitted making a mistake. |

---

# 22. Reporting Verbs + Infinitive

Some reporting verbs are followed by:

> **to + verb**

| Verb | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **agree** | agree + to + verb | He agreed **to help**. |
| **offer** | offer + to + verb | She offered **to drive**. |
| **promise** | promise + to + verb | He promised **to call**. |
| **refuse** | refuse + to + verb | She refused **to answer**. |
| **decide** | decide + to + verb | They decided **to leave**. |
| **threaten** | threaten + to + verb | He threatened **to complain**. |

---

# 23. Reporting Verbs + -ing

Some reporting verbs are followed by a **gerund (-ing)**.

| Verb | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **suggest** | suggest + -ing | She suggested **going home**. |
| **admit** | admit + -ing | He admitted **making a mistake**. |
| **deny** | deny + -ing | She denied **taking the money**. |
| **recommend** | recommend + -ing | He recommended **booking early**. |
| **apologize for** | apologize for + -ing | She apologized for **being late**. |

### Important

> ❌ She suggested **to go** home.

> ✅ She suggested **going** home.

---

# 24. When Tense Does Not Need to Change

The tense does not always need to change.

### General Truth

Direct:

> The teacher said, **"Water boils at 100°C."**

Reported:

> The teacher said that **water boils at 100°C**.

Because this is a general scientific fact, the present tense can remain.

### Still True

Direct:

> She said, **"I live in Hanoi."**

If she still lives there:

> She said that she **lives in Hanoi**.

---

# 25. Reporting Verb in the Present

When the reporting verb is in the **present**, backshift is usually not necessary.

### Direct

> "I am tired."

### Reported

> He **says that he is tired**.

| Reporting Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| **says** | She says that she **is** busy. |
| **tells** | He tells me that he **needs** help. |
| **asks** | She asks where I **live**. |

# 27. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| He said me that he was tired. | He **told me** that he was tired. | Use **tell + person**. |
| He told that he was tired. | He **said that** he was tired. | **Tell** needs an object. |
| She asked me **where did I live**. | She asked me **where I lived**. | Reported questions use statement word order. |
| He asked **that I was ready**. | He asked **if I was ready**. | Yes/no questions use **if/whether**. |
| She told me **don't go**. | She told me **not to go**. | Negative commands use **not to + verb**. |
| He suggested **to go** home. | He suggested **going** home. | Suggest is normally followed by **-ing**. |
| She said that she **is** tired. | She said that she **was** tired. | Backshift is normally used with a past reporting verb when appropriate. |

# 28. How to Change Direct Speech into Reported Speech

### Step 1: Identify the type

Is it:

- A statement?
- A yes/no question?
- A WH-question?
- A command?
- A request?
- A suggestion?

### Step 2: Choose the reporting verb

> said, told, asked, advised, suggested, etc.

### Step 3: Change pronouns

> I → he/she  
> my → his/her  
> you → me/him/her/etc.

### Step 4: Change the tense if necessary

> am → was  
> have → had  
> will → would

### Step 5: Change time/place words if necessary

> today → that day  
> tomorrow → the next day  
> here → there

### Example

Direct:

> Mary said, "I will meet you here tomorrow."

Reported:

> Mary said that **she would meet me there the next day**.

## Key Point

**Reported speech** tells us what someone said without repeating their exact words.

### Main Patterns

> **Statement:**  
> He said **that he was tired**.

> **Yes/No Question:**  
> She asked **if I was ready**.

> **WH-Question:**  
> He asked **where I lived**.

> **Command:**  
> She told me **to sit down**.

> **Negative Command:**  
> He told me **not to touch it**.

> **Request:**  
> She asked me **to help her**.

> **Suggestion:**  
> He suggested **going home**.

### Remember

> **Statements:** said/told + clause

> **Questions:** asked + if/whether/WH-word + statement order

> **Commands:** told + object + to + verb

> **Negative commands:** told + object + not to + verb

> **Suggestions:** suggested + -ing
