# Present Tense Summary

## 1. Present Tenses Overview

| Tense | Structure | Main Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | S + V1/V-s | Habits, routines, facts, states | She **works** every day. |
| **Present Continuous** | S + am/is/are + V-ing | Actions happening now, temporary situations | She **is working** now. |
| **Present Perfect** | S + have/has + V3 | Past actions connected to the present | She **has finished** her work. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | S + have/has been + V-ing | Actions continuing until now; duration | She **has been working** for two hours. |

---

## 2. Present Simple

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | S + V1/V-s | She **works** here. |
| **Negative** | S + do/does not + V1 | She **doesn't work** here. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Do/Does + S + V1? | **Does** she **work** here? |
| **WH-Question** | WH + do/does + S + V1? | Where **does** she **work**? |

### Uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Habit | I **exercise** every morning. |
| Routine | She **goes** to work at 8 a.m. |
| General fact | Water **boils** at 100°C. |
| Permanent situation | He **lives** in London. |
| Repeated action | They **visit** us every Sunday. |
| Timetable | The train **leaves** at 7 p.m. |
| State | I **know** the answer. |

### Common Signal Words

| Signal Words |
|---|
| always |
| usually |
| often |
| sometimes |
| rarely |
| never |
| every day |
| every week |
| on Mondays |

### Important Rule

| Subject | Verb |
|---|---|
| I/You/We/They | work |
| He/She/It | works |

After **does/doesn't**, use **V1**.

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| Does she **works** here? | Does she **work** here? |
| She doesn't **works** here. | She doesn't **work** here. |

## 5. Present Perfect Continuous

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | S + have/has been + V-ing | She **has been working**. |
| **Negative** | S + have/has not been + V-ing | She **hasn't been working**. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Have/Has + S + been + V-ing? | **Has** she **been working**? |
| **WH-Question** | How long + have/has + S + been + V-ing? | How long **has** she **been working**? |

### Uses

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Action continuing until now | I **have been studying** for two hours. |
| Emphasis on duration | They **have been waiting** since 8 a.m. |
| Recent activity with present evidence | She is tired because she **has been running**. |
| Repeated recent activity | He **has been working** a lot lately. |

## 6. Present Tenses Comparison

| Tense | Main Idea | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Present Simple** | Habit / fact / routine | I **work** every day. |
| **Present Continuous** | Happening now / temporary | I **am working** now. |
| **Present Perfect** | Past + present connection | I **have worked** today. |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Duration until now | I **have been working** for two hours. |

---

## 7. Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|---|---|
| Habit | Happening now |
| Routine | Temporary situation |
| General fact | Current activity |
| Permanent situation | Changing situation |
| I **work** every day. | I **am working** now. |
| She **lives** in Hanoi. | She **is living** with her friend this month. |

---

## 8. Present Perfect vs. Present Perfect Continuous

| Present Perfect | Present Perfect Continuous |
|---|---|
| Focus on result | Focus on activity / duration |
| I **have painted** the room. | I **have been painting** the room. |
| She **has written** five emails. | She **has been writing** emails for two hours. |
| Focus on what is completed | Focus on how long / activity |

---

## 9. Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|---|---|
| Time is not specified | Specific finished time |
| Connection to present | Finished past |
| already / just / yet | yesterday / ago |
| ever / never | last year |
| since / for | in 2020 |

### Examples

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|---|---|
| I **have visited** Japan. | I **visited** Japan in 2020. |
| She **has finished** her work. | She **finished** her work yesterday. |
| Have you **seen** this movie? | Did you **see** this movie last night? |

---

## 10. Since vs. For

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **since** | Starting point | I have lived here **since 2020**. |
| **for** | Duration | I have lived here **for five years**. |

### Since

| Starting Point |
|---|
| since 2020 |
| since Monday |
| since January |
| since 8 a.m. |
| since I was a child |

### For

| Duration |
|---|
| for two hours |
| for three days |

| for a long time |

## 11. Already, Just, Yet

| Word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **already** | Earlier than expected | I have **already** finished. |
| **just** | Very recently | She has **just** arrived. |
| **yet** | Until now; questions | Have you finished **yet**? |
| **yet** | Until now; negatives | I haven't finished **yet**. |

## 15. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| She **work** every day. | She **works** every day. | He/she/it → V-s |
| Does she **works** here? | Does she **work** here? | After does → V1 |
| She is **work** now. | She is **working** now. | Be + V-ing |
| I have **went** there. | I have **gone** there. | Have/has + V3 |
| I have lived here **since five years**. | I have lived here **for five years**. | for + duration |
| I have lived here **for 2020**. | I have lived here **since 2020**. | since + starting point |
| I am **knowing** the answer. | I **know** the answer. | Stative verbs usually use simple forms |

## 16. Stative Verbs

| Category | Common Verbs |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | know, understand, remember, forget |
| Opinion | believe, think, suppose |
| Emotion | like, love, hate, prefer |
| Desire | want, need |
| Possession | own, belong, have |
| Other states | seem, contain, include, depend |

| Correct | Usually Incorrect |
|---|---|
| I **know** the answer. | I am knowing the answer. |
| She **likes** coffee. | She is liking coffee. |
